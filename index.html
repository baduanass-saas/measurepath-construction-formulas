01. 2D Boolean Floor & Wall Grid Engine

The core spatial layout engine handles rectangular, L-shaped, and custom room polygons by calculating gross wall perimeters and applying Boolean geometric subtractions for architectural doors and windows.

Gross Wall Area: 2 × (Length + Width) × Ceiling Height
Net Paintable Area: Gross Wall Area − ∑(Door Deductions + Window Deductions)
Ceiling Area: Length × Width

Standard architectural deductions: 21 sq ft (1.95 m²) per standard 3×7 ft interior door; 12 to 15 sq ft (1.11 to 1.39 m²) per window. Test this engine live in the Room Paint & Primer Calculator → or model your walls in real time in the 2D/3D Takeoff Studio →.

02. Roof Pitch & Slope Multiplier Formula

Converts a 2D building footprint area into true 3D sloping roof deck area for asphalt shingles, metal panels, and underlayment rolls using Pythagorean rafter slope geometry.

Pitch Ratio: Rise ÷ Run (standardized to X : 12)
Pitch Multiplier: √(1 + (Rise ÷ Run)²)
Pitch Angle (θ): arctan(Rise ÷ Run) × (180 ÷ π)
Slope Gradient (%): (Rise ÷ Run) × 100
True Roof Surface: Flat Footprint Area × Pitch Multiplier

03. 1 Quart vs. 1 Gallon Paint Coverage Rates

Based on ASTM D5150 architectural coating spread rates on primed interior drywall:

Gallons Required: (Net Wall Area ÷ Spread Rate) × Coats × (1 + Waste Factor)
1 US Gallon (3.785 L): 350 to 400 sq ft (32.5 to 37.2 m²) @ 1 coat | 175 to 200 sq ft @ 2 coats
1 US Quart (0.946 L): 85 to 100 sq ft (7.9 to 9.3 m²) @ 1 coat | 45 to 50 sq ft @ 2 coats
8 oz Sample Jar (0.237 L): 20 to 25 sq ft (1.9 to 2.3 m²) for color swatches

04. Concrete Cubic Yards, Slab Cost & Bag Yields

Adheres to American Concrete Institute (ACI 302) volume guidelines for slabs, footings, and cylindrical sonotube piers:

Cubic Yards (yd³): [Length (ft) × Width (ft) × (Thickness (in) ÷ 12)] ÷ 27 × (1 + Waste)
80 lb Concrete Bag Yield: 0.60 cu ft → 45 bags per cubic yard
60 lb Concrete Bag Yield: 0.45 cu ft → 60 bags per cubic yard
40 lb Concrete Bag Yield: 0.30 cu ft → 90 bags per cubic yard
Complete Slab Cost: (Volume × Ready-Mix Price) + (Area × Labor/sq ft) + Prep + Rebar + Delivery

05. Drywall Sheets, Joint Mud & Fastener Takeoff
